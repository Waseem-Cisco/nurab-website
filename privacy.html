Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Nurab is a free app that shows prayer times and the jamaat times of mosques near you, shared by the community. This policy explains, in plain words, what information the app collects, why, and what control you have. Nurab is run by Syed Mohmed Waseem, an individual developer in India. Questions: nurab.app@gmail.com.

In short: we collect only what the app needs, we never sell your data, there are no ads and no tracking, and you can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Your profile, if you give it: name, gender, mobile number and city, and whether you'd like Ramadan and Eid updates. Your mobile number is confirmed with a one-time code.
  • What you add: mosques, mosque names, jamaat timings, answers such as whether a mosque has space for ladies, votes on whether timings are correct, timing suggestions, reports of problems, requests to manage a mosque (with the role and contact number you give), and mosque photos you send.
  • Searches that find nothing: when you search for a mosque and none is found, we keep what you typed and the rough area (to about 1 km) for 6 months, so missing mosques can be added.
  • An app account ID: the app creates an anonymous ID when it first opens, so your settings and contributions can be linked to you without a password.
  • Your location: where you are when you open the app and when you change a mosque. See "Your location" below.

What stays on your phone only

Your namaz tracker (which prayers you ticked), tasbih counts, the duas you saved, and your settings are stored only on your phone. We don't receive them.

Your location

  • The app uses your location on your phone to work out prayer times, the Qibla direction, Sehri and Iftar, and to find nearby mosques.
  • We save your location when you open the app (then at most every 10 minutes or when you've moved, while the app is open) and just before you add or change a mosque's details, send a suggestion or send a photo. This lets us check that changes are made by people at the mosque, and stop fake or careless edits. With each change we also record how far from the mosque it was made.
  • This applies whether or not you have made a profile. If you don't allow location access, nothing is saved.
  • In the European Union, the EEA (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we don't save your location at all. The app still uses it on your phone for prayer times, the Qibla and nearby mosques, but it isn't sent to us, and no distance from the mosque is recorded with your changes. The app goes by the country your phone is in, or by your phone's region setting when it doesn't know your location.
  • Saved locations are visible only to the app's admin, never to other users, and are deleted automatically after 90 days, or straight away if you delete your account.
  • If you turn on "Jamaat soon" alerts, your phone watches for you passing near mosques, even when the app is closed. This happens on your phone; those background checks are not sent to us. You can turn it off in Settings.

Photos you scan

When you scan a mosque's timing board or a dua, the picture is sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude AI service, which reads the text and sends it back. We don't keep the picture after reading it. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on this data.

Why we use it

  • To show mosques and jamaat times, and to let you add and correct them.
  • To keep timings trustworthy: only people with a confirmed mobile number can change shared information, and we can undo edits and block misuse.
  • To contact you about a mosque you asked to manage.
  • Only if you turn it on: to send Ramadan and Eid updates.

Who can see what

  • Everyone using the app sees mosque details, jamaat timings, vote counts and approved mosque photos. Your name and number are not shown with them.
  • The app's admin can see profiles, who made each change and from how far away, and saved locations, to keep the information correct and stop misuse.
  • Mosque photos are checked by our team before anyone else can see them.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the app's data and photos, on servers in Mumbai, India.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads the pictures you scan, as described above.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) delivers the one-time code that confirms your mobile number.
  • Expo delivers app updates; your phone checks with Expo's servers for a newer version.
  • Overture Maps, OpenStreetMap and Foursquare Open Source Places provide the mosque locations. No personal data is sent to them.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking services, and we never sell or rent your data.

How long we keep it

Your profile and account stay until you delete your account. Saved locations are deleted after 90 days. Scanned pictures are not kept. Mosques, timings and approved photos you added stay in the app after you delete your account, because others rely on them, but they are no longer linked to you.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile at any time in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete my account. See how account deletion works.
  • Turn location, notifications and alerts on or off in Settings or your phone's settings.
  • Email nurab.app@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access rules on our database mean people can only change what they're allowed to, and photos waiting for review can't be opened by anyone except the sender and our team.

Children

Nurab is meant for a general audience and is not designed for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has given us their details, email us and we'll delete them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
